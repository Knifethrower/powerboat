Default mouse pointers of HTML pages (powerboat/js/86c_cursors.js)

The arrow, hand, text, wait, grab and not-allowed pointers are images from GNOME's Adwaita cursor theme
(adwaita-icon-theme 46.0), converted from X cursor files to PNG by tools/xcursor_to_js.py. Modified: colors
inverted (white fill, black outline; the drop shadow unchanged). These modified images are shared under the
same terms.
Source: https://download.gnome.org/sources/adwaita-icon-theme/
